= Final Challenge

A mixed cluster has three problems:

* An iLO host selects the wrong provisioning MAC.
* An iDRAC host cannot mount the ISO.
* Registered hosts remain blocked by validation failures.

== Tasks

. Inspect normalized BMC evidence.
. Configure a deterministic NIC selector.
. Validate ISO reachability from the BMC network.
. Review virtual-media categories and resource IDs.
. Inspect Assisted Installer events and static-network mappings.
. Repeat read-only discovery.
. Repeat the one-host virtual-media test.
. Resume installation only after all blocking conditions are understood.

== Completion Criteria

* Both vendors pass discovery and virtual-media tests.
* All MAC mappings are deterministic.
* All blocking validations are resolved.
* Secrets and artifacts remain outside Git.
