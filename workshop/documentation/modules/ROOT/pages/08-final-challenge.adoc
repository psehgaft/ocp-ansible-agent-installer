= 7.1 Final Challenge

This challenge validates the complete workshop path, from workstation preparation and Redfish discovery through OpenShift installation, ordered Day-2 configuration, health checks, and ACM-based cluster deployment.

== Scenario

You are responsible for recovering and standardizing a mixed-vendor OpenShift environment. The current state includes:

* An iLO host selecting the wrong provisioning MAC.
* An iDRAC host unable to mount the discovery ISO.
* Assisted Installer hosts blocked by validation failures.
* Inconsistent NTP sources across MachineConfigPools.
* Worker nodes missing approved infrastructure and workload labels.
* An incomplete LDAP and HTPasswd identity configuration.
* Platform operators in mixed readiness states.
* OADP configured without a verified BackupStorageLocation.
* Health-check evidence showing unresolved warnings.
* An ACM cluster template that has not passed validation.

== Part 1: Foundations and security

. Validate the control-node dependencies and Python environment.
. Confirm the active inventory contains the intended HPE and Dell systems.
. Validate cluster, network, and profile variables.
. Confirm that no active `CHANGE_ME` placeholders remain.
. Verify that Vault data is encrypted and secrets are excluded from Git.
. Select either `direct` or `gitops` mode and explain why it is appropriate.

== Part 2: Redfish and Assisted Installer

. Run read-only BMC discovery against all target hosts.
. Inspect normalized hardware, firmware, NIC, and provisioning-MAC evidence.
. Configure a deterministic NIC selector or explicit MAC override for the affected iLO host.
. Validate ISO reachability from the BMC network.
. Run the virtual-media test against one non-production host by using `--limit`.
. Verify one-time boot behavior and eject the test media.
. Review Assisted Installer events and resolve blocking host validations.

== Part 3: OpenShift installation

. Run repository and installation preflight checks.
. Generate or retrieve the discovery ISO.
. Boot the mixed-vendor hosts with virtual media.
. Verify that all hosts register with the expected hostname, MAC address, and role.
. Start the installation only after all blocking validations are resolved.
. Verify `ClusterVersion`, nodes, and cluster operators.
. Eject virtual media from every installed node.

== Part 4: Ordered Day-2 foundation

. Configure approved NTP servers for the required MachineConfigPools.
. Wait until the affected MachineConfigPools report `UPDATED=True` and `DEGRADED=False`.
. Apply approved node labels, including infrastructure-node labels where required.
. Verify node readiness, workload placement, and protected-label policy enforcement.
. Configure LDAP or another enterprise IDP.
. Configure the emergency HTPasswd administrator identity.
. Validate the OAuth Operator and test both login paths.

== Part 5: Platform operators

Validate or deploy the following components in the documented sequence:

. ACM.
. ACS.
. Storage operators and storage classes:
.. LVM Storage Operator.
.. Local Storage Operator.
.. ODF.
.. The selected Pure Storage, HPE, or NetApp CSI driver.
. OpenShift Virtualization.
. MTV.
. AAP.
. Cert Manager and PKI resources.
. Quay and registry integration.
. Networking resources:
.. NMState.
.. Multus.
.. SR-IOV Network Operator.
. Monitoring and Logging.
. OADP, including the Operator CSV, DataProtectionApplication, BackupStorageLocation, and a test backup.
. Cluster Observability Operator.
. Network Observability Operator.
. Bare Metal Operator.
. Load Balancer Operator.
. RHOSO Operator, when included in the selected profile.

For every component, record:

* The playbook or GitOps path used.
* Required variables.
* Operator namespace and subscription channel.
* Readiness evidence.
* Rollback or remediation notes.

== Part 6: Health check and extensibility

. Run the cluster health and reporting workflow.
. Review critical, warning, and informational findings.
. Remediate at least one actionable warning and rerun the relevant check.
. Add one small component to the framework by following the component-extension contract.
. Render its desired state and verify the generated artifact.
. Confirm that repository, schema, lint, and documentation validations pass.

== Part 7: Deploy a cluster from ACM

. Generate the managed-cluster template.
. Generate its network definition.
. Validate all required variables and rendered YAML.
. Run server-side validation before deployment.
. Deploy the cluster through the selected direct or GitOps workflow.
. Verify `ManagedCluster`, `ClusterDeployment`, `AgentClusterInstall`, and Agent status.
. Confirm that the cluster becomes available and joins ACM successfully.

== Completion criteria

The challenge is complete only when:

* Both hardware vendors pass discovery and virtual-media validation.
* MAC mappings and network definitions are deterministic.
* The OpenShift installation is healthy and all nodes are Ready.
* NTP, node labels, identity providers, and OAuth are validated.
* Required platform operators are installed or explicitly marked out of scope.
* OADP completes a test backup and reports an available storage location.
* Health-check findings have documented dispositions.
* The new framework component passes repository quality gates.
* The ACM-managed cluster template validates and the cluster reaches an available state.
* Secrets and generated sensitive artifacts remain outside Git.

== Evidence package

Store the following evidence under the designated artifacts directory:

* Inventory and preflight output.
* Redfish discovery reports.
* Assisted Installer events.
* Installation verification output.
* MachineConfigPool and node-label evidence.
* OAuth and identity-provider status.
* Operator CSV and custom-resource readiness output.
* OADP backup evidence.
* Health-check reports.
* ACM rendered templates and deployment status.
* Final remediation summary.
