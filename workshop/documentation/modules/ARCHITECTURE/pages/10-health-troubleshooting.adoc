= Health Checks, Reporting, and Troubleshooting

Run upgrade prechecks, certificate audit, etcd health, component health checks, and consolidated reporting as evidence-driven workflows.

[source,bash]
----
ansible-playbook playbooks/day2/upgrade-prechecks.yml
ansible-playbook playbooks/day2/certificate-audit.yml
ansible-playbook playbooks/day2/etcd-health.yml
ansible-playbook playbooks/day2/health-checks.yml
ansible-playbook playbooks/generate-component-report.yml
----

Every component should publish a normalized `component_result` with requested state, effective state, deployment mode, version, installation readiness, functional checks, warnings, errors, remediation, and evidence.

Troubleshooting order:

. Validate inventory and profile resolution.
. Inspect dependency plan and compatibility checks.
. Validate rendered YAML and Kustomize output.
. Check operator Subscription, CSV, workloads, and operand conditions.
. Review Argo CD sync, health, waves, and project permissions.
. Collect precise evidence before changing configuration.

Reports are generated as Markdown, JSON, HTML, and CSV. Evidence must never contain credentials or unredacted Secret values.
